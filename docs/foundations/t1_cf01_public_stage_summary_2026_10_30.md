## 1. What is the project asking?

The project's broad question is whether causal order and quantum amplitudes can support an account of 3+1-dimensional spacetime and massive matter without presupposing a spacetime background. The answer in this repository remains unknown. In the Stage 5C bookkeeping, events, strict order and counting are inputs; global two-order sector data are restricted derived bookkeeping, while complex amplitudes, pairings and other quantum scaffolding require explicit provenance. A metric, a calibrated clock or a continuum propagator supplied for comparison cannot also be counted as an emergent output. The restricted Stage 5C target concerns 1+1-dimensional massless two-channel propagation; it is not the broad 3+1-dimensional matter/gravity claim. This restates [README, “What this is”](../../README.md), [roadmap §1](../THEORY_FIRST_ROADMAP_DRAFT.md) and [T1 §1](t1_t5_premise_audit.md), as present at the reference snapshot; it does not extend their aims.

The intended reader is a researcher who can identify an antecedent, an invalid inference, or a missing assumption. The tables below are a guide to the arguments and their limits, not a replacement for them.
